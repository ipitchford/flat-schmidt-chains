# Source and priority scope

Search/access date: 28 September 2026.
Requested basis: Problem 5 in the supplied `Pasted text(20260928-171058).txt`.
That problem proposes a deterministic partial map of rank-one qutrit
frustration-free chains, with certified gapped regions and explicit gapless
families, while identifying rank-one and rank-two qutrit projectors as the
broader Bravyi–Gosset target.

## Primary sources used

Bravyi, S., & Gosset, D. (2015). Gapped and gapless phases of frustration-free
spin-1/2 chains. Journal of Mathematical Physics, 56, 061902.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.04035

This source states the qubit dichotomy and explicitly proposes d=3 with local
projector ranks r=1,2 as a next case. Its XXZ examples provide context for
one-particle tridiagonal spectra. The manuscript supplies its own marker proof.

Lemm, M. (2019). Gaplessness is not generic for translation-invariant spin
chains. Physical Review B, 100, 035113.
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.100.035113
https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.00108

The deterministic neighborhood statement appears on printed page 5;
Theorems 5 and 6 on printed page 6 give deterministic three-site criteria.
Those PDF pages were inspected. This contradicts the brief's implication
that prior progress was exclusively probabilistic. General deterministic
finite-size certification is therefore explicitly treated as prior work.

Hunter-Jones, N., & Lemm, M. (2025). Two classes of quantum spin systems that
are gapped on any bounded-degree graph. Preprint.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22438

The title in the source brief is not the current arXiv title. The paper treats
random rank-one projections at large local dimension and a second class of
Haar-projector models, using generalized finite-size criteria. It is not a
complete deterministic d=3 classification.

Rai, K. S., Kull, I., Emonts, P., Tura, J., Schuch, N., & Baccari, F. (2026).
A hierarchy of spectral gap certificates for frustration-free spin systems.
Quantum, 10, 2065.
https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2026-04-13-2065

The publication date and metadata were checked against the journal page.
The hierarchy includes finite-size methods. This bundle does not claim to
have implemented the general hierarchy or improved its general performance.

Bishop, M. R. (2017). Spectral gaps for the Two-Species Product Vacua and
Boundary States models on the d-dimensional lattice. Preprint consulted.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.04755

Relevant as a contrast, not as a theorem applicable to the present rank-two
hopping-only model. The excluded interaction terms and different ground
spaces are stated in the paper and internal audit.

## Novelty boundary

Targeted searches also considered quantum clocks, monomer–dimer and hard-core
heat-bath representations, pair-flip/rewrite chains, and particle-dependent
exclusion dynamics. They did not establish historical priority for the
specific locally isospectral separation or for the two-species bottleneck.
The manuscript therefore presents complete arguments without claiming that
all ingredients or each component example first appear here. A specialist
review should compare the exact theorem statements, not only their keywords.

or incorporated into this release.
