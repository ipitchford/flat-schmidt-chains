# Identical local spectra, different quantum-chain gap phases

**Research candidate; unrefereed — 28 September 2026**

Two nearest-neighbor qutrit chains can have exactly the same two-site and
three-site energy spectra, and the same local Schmidt coefficients, while
one remains uniformly gapped and the other's gap closes as the chain grows.
The manuscript supplies explicit rank-one interactions realizing this
separation, with a positive gap bound of 1/4 for the gapped model and the
exact formula 1 − cos(π/N) for the gapless model. They are endpoints of a
continuous path on which all the stated local spectral data stay unchanged.

The analysis combines an exactly solvable moving-marker sector with a
monomer–dimer representation of the other chain. An independent rational
four-site certificate proves a whole neighborhood to be gapped, including
an explicit full-Schmidt-rank interaction that is not certified by the
usual three-site test. A rank-two construction adds a different mechanism:
two noninterchanging species with opposite biases create an exponentially
small gap even though each constituent rank-one chain is uniformly gapped.

The result is a deterministic partial map of the qutrit problem suggested
by Bravyi and Gosset, not a classification of all qutrit projectors.
Deterministic finite-size gap criteria are established prior work and are
not presented as new. The accompanying bundle contains complete analytic
proofs, exact finite certificates, reproducible verifiers, and a clear
separation between proof and numerical diagnostics. The candidate was
developed with AI assistance; historical priority and the proofs remain
subject to independent specialist review.
