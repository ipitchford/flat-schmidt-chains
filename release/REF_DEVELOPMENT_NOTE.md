# Research development and REF assessment boundary

**28 September 2026 — version 1.0.** This note records the change in the research contribution. It is not a new referee report, an official REF assessment or a claim that the output has attained four stars.

## Starting assessment

The supplied review assessed version 0.1 as a credible specialist contribution, provisionally at 3* with uncertainty at the 2*/3* boundary. It identified significance and the precise originality claim as the main obstacles to a higher assessment. It found no fatal mathematical defect and asked for better positioning against qubit and Motzkin antecedents. That assessment is preserved verbatim in the bundle.

## What changed substantively

The revised paper no longer depends on presenting an isolated pair as its main result. It proves a necessary-and-sufficient classification for every rank-one interaction with exactly two equal nonzero Schmidt probabilities. In this class, all gapless interactions are balanced markers and have an exact finite-size gap; every other interaction has a proved uniform positive gap. The difficult intersecting-support case has a quantitative four-site proof. The higher-dimensional statement follows by support geometry and spectator reduction rather than by an unsupported extrapolation from qutrit calculations.

The previously open interpolation is now classified for every parameter. More strongly, it is the full on-site-unitary normal form of the specified qutrit short-spectrum fibre. A small analytic certificate gives a uniform bound $\cos^2\theta/6$ throughout its gapped part. Its finite-length ground-space dimension remains constant at the gapless endpoint.

These are changes in theorem scope and explanatory power, not additional polishing or larger numerical runs. They answer two research extensions specifically suggested by the referee. The exact-input classification utility makes the decision rule executable, within its algebraic domain.

## How this bears on the quality dimensions

**Originality.** The revised originality claim concerns the complete flat-Schmidt-rank-two theorem, the compatibility argument at the critical overlap threshold, and the entire fibre classification. It does not claim the general three-site gap method, the unrestricted local-spectral obstruction, the unbiased hopping formula or the Motzkin components as new. A targeted source search did not identify an equivalent classification. That is bounded search evidence, not a priority proof.

**Significance.** A complete class-level dichotomy is more substantial than the previous examples. It identifies information that short spectra omit: how neighbouring extremal overlap vectors can fit together. The four-site argument may be useful elsewhere, but no such wider application or field-level influence is claimed. The class is constrained, and the full arbitrary-qutrit question remains outside the result.

**Rigour.** The paper supplies analytic all-length proofs, exact finite positivity certificates, separate numerical implementations, provenance-preserving reruns and a claim-level audit. The new proof remains unrefereed and is not formalised in a proof assistant. Those qualifications cannot be replaced by test counts.

## Four-star aspiration

The published generic REF definition distinguishes world-leading quality at 4* from internationally excellent quality at 3*, judged through originality, significance and rigour [1]. The revision materially strengthens the contribution on which such a judgement would be made. It does **not** establish that the world-leading threshold has been met. No numerical aggregation of diagnostic scores is used here.

The current REF 2029 timetable consulted on 28 September 2026 schedules final panel criteria for autumn 2026 [2]. This work did not establish a final Mathematical Sciences-specific rubric; none is invented. The generic published definition is used only to explain the aspiration.

A renewed external review should concentrate on the universal validity of Lemmas 4.1 and 4.2, the normal-form completeness in Theorem 5.1, and equivalent results in the nearest-neighbour projector literature. Subject to those checks, version 1.0 is a materially stronger candidate for assessment than version 0.1. Calling it “four-star research” now would go beyond the evidence.
