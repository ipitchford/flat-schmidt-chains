# Source and prior-art audit

**Search/inspection date:** 28 September 2026. **Purpose:** action the supplied review, check exact model relationships, and test the candidate classification's positioning. This is a targeted audit, not a systematic or exhaustive literature review.

External definitions and relevant theorem statements were inspected, including PDF equation pages for free Motzkin and the deformed Motzkin model. ## Search questions

Live web and Exa searches asked for rank-one qutrit spectral-gap classifications; flat rank-two Schmidt/partial-isometry interactions; support-subspace and critical-overlap saturation criteria; locally isospectral gapped/gapless projector pairs; free/deformed Motzkin hopping-only chains; and opposing-bias conserved-order exclusion processes. Searches also tested possible zero-range-process lower bounds as an alternative extension. No applicable general lower bound was imported without its hypotheses; that route did not produce a matching full-chain rank-two asymptotic theorem.

Some short keyword searches returned irrelevant retail “Gap” results. Those results were excluded and are not counted as evidence of absence. Semantically targeted searches returned the primary papers listed above and related finite-size-criterion literature. No exact equivalent of the new flat-Schmidt classification was located in the inspected material.

## Result-by-result attribution

**Established method or direct prior consequence:** sequential product ground states; adjacent-projector overlap estimates; the qubit flat-Schmidt gaplessness conclusion; deterministic neighbourhood methodology; the fact that the unrestricted short-spectrum obstruction follows from the qubit classification; unbiased free-Motzkin/hopping reduction.

**Retained candidate contributions from version 0.1:** the exact marker family in the stated generality; all-positive-weight dimer bound; explicit rank-maximality-based complex neighbourhood certificate; quantitative opposing-bias exponential upper bound. The fact that a local component already appeared in Motzkin work is not treated as proof that each of these theorems was previously proved.

**New candidate contributions in version 1.0:** the complete flat-Schmidt-rank-two dichotomy and quantitative intersecting-support obstruction; the complete short-spectrum fibre normal form; its uniform $\cos^2\theta/6$ lower bound; and its fixed-degeneracy refinement. Equivalent earlier formulations may exist outside the inspected corpus. Historical priority remains unconfirmed.
